How gasless works

Zero fees. Same security.

You sign, we sponsor the fee. Your swap settles on Solana exactly as before.

1
You sign an intent

Instead of a fee-paying transaction, you sign a message authorizing the swap. No SOL needed.

2
Nova sponsors the fee

Our relayer co-signs as fee payer in a versioned transaction and submits it to the Solana network.

3
You receive your tokens

The swap routes through the same on-chain liquidity. Settlement takes about 400 milliseconds.

Gasless swaps use Solana fee-payer sponsorship and are available on all supported clusters.
