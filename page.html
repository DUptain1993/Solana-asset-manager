How gasless works

Zero gas. Same security.

You sign, we relay. Your swap settles on-chain exactly as before.

1
You sign an intent

Instead of a gas-paying transaction, you sign an EIP-712 message authorizing the swap.

2
Nova relays it on-chain

Our relayer submits your signed intent to the settlement contract and covers the gas cost.

3
You receive your tokens

The swap executes against the same on-chain liquidity. No ETH needed in your wallet.

Gasless swaps use ERC-2771 meta-transactions and are available on all supported networks.
